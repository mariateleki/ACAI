# How Are U.S. Universities Responding to AI? An Audit of Governance Capacity

This repository contains: 

- **ACAI-US79 Dataset**  
  Human-annotated governance indicators for 79 U.S. universities, derived from publicly accessible institutional materials.

- **Audit Instrument & Annotation Schema**  
  The full set of governance statements, classification rules, and annotation guidelines used by human annotators.

- **ACAI Index Definition**  
  Formal definition of the Academic AI Capacity Index, including aggregation rules, weighting schemes, and robustness checks.

- **Supporting Materials**  
  Documentation supporting transparency, re-auditing, critique, and institutional self-assessment.

A public, interactive companion website is available at:  
👉 **https://acai-us79.org/**

## Methodology (High-Level)

- Time-bounded (≤5 minutes per item) review of institutionally authoritative public materials
- Three independent human annotators per institution
- Categorical scoring: Present (1.0), Partial (0.5), Absent/Unclear/Conflicting (0.0)
- Aggregation preserves annotator disagreement as a signal of governance ambiguity

## Intended Use

This repository is designed to support:

- Re-audits and counter-audits
- Longitudinal tracking of institutional governance changes
- Comparative research on AI governance in higher education
- Institutional self-assessment and accountability work

Disagreement with annotations or index design is expected and encouraged.
